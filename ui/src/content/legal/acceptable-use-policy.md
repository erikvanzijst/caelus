## 2. Prohibited content

You must not use the Service to upload, store, host, transmit, distribute, or
link to any content that:

2.1 **Is unlawful**, including: child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content
that sexually exploits or endangers minors; content that promotes, incites, or
facilitates terrorism or violent extremism; content that infringes any
intellectual-property right (including copyright, trademark, or trade secret);
content that is defamatory, libellous, or constitutes unlawful hate speech under
applicable law; or content that violates export-control or sanctions laws.

2.2 **Violates privacy or data-protection law**, including unlawfully obtained
personal data, doxxing, or non-consensual intimate imagery.

2.3 **Is fraudulent or deceptive**, including phishing pages, fake or
impersonating sites, or content designed to deceive users into disclosing
credentials or making payments.

2.4 **Is malicious**, including malware, ransomware, viruses, worms, trojans,
exploit kits, or command-and-control infrastructure.

## 3. Prohibited conduct and uses

You must not use the Service to:

3.1 **Send spam or bulk unsolicited messages**, or operate mailing infrastructure
in violation of anti-spam laws.

3.2 **Attack or interfere with any system or network**, including denial-of-
service (DoS/DDoS) attacks, unauthorised port scanning, penetration attempts,
intrusion, or any attempt to gain unauthorised access to data, accounts, or
systems (whether ours or a third party's).

3.3 **Abuse Service resources**, including: unauthorised cryptocurrency mining;
deliberately circumventing usage limits, quotas, or billing; or consuming
resources in a manner that degrades the Service for other customers
("noisy-neighbour" abuse) beyond the limits of your plan.

3.4 **Resell, sublicense, or provide the Service to third parties** except as
expressly permitted by your plan.

3.5 **Use the Service for high-risk applications** where failure could lead to
death, personal injury, or severe environmental or property damage (e.g. life-
support, nuclear, aircraft, or emergency-response systems), unless expressly
agreed in writing. The Service is not designed or warranted for such uses.

3.6 **Misrepresent your identity** or forge headers/identifiers to disguise the
origin of content or traffic.

4.1 You must keep your Instances, applications, and dependencies reasonably
secure and up to date, and must not knowingly run vulnerable software that

you are responsible for operating them lawfully — including providing any

5.1 Reports of content or conduct violating this AUP, or of illegal content, may
be sent to abuse@freepod.eu. The notice-and-action process in Section 9
of the Terms governs handling of illegal-content notices.

5.2 We encourage rights-holders and authorities to provide sufficiently detailed
notices (location/URL, nature of the violation, and contact details) so we can
act diligently.

6.1 **Our rights.** We may investigate suspected violations and, in our
reasonable discretion and proportionate to the violation: (a) remove or disable
access to content; (b) throttle, suspend, or terminate the Service or an
Instance; (c) suspend or close the account; and (d) take any action required by
law.

6.2 **Severity-based response.** For severe violations (e.g. CSAM, active
attacks, content posing imminent harm), we may act **immediately and without
prior notice**, including immediate suspension and reporting to authorities. For
less severe violations, we will, where practicable, give you notice and an
opportunity to cure.

6.3 **Cooperation with authorities.** We may preserve, disclose, and report
content and account information to law-enforcement or other competent
authorities where required by law or a valid legal request, and we may be
legally obliged to report certain content (such as CSAM).

6.4 **No refunds for enforcement.** Suspension or termination for an AUP
violation does not entitle you to a refund, except where mandatory consumer law
requires otherwise.

6.5 **Statement of reasons.** Where required by the Digital Services Act or other
law, we will provide affected users with a statement of reasons and information
about available redress mechanisms.
